**How long it takes / four ways it dies:**
1. `lifecycle.py:2252-2253` — waits until `nao_antes_de` = `carimbo_manual + 30.0` (`:2195`). So the earliest drain is **30 s after the manual gesture**, plus up to 1 s of tick granularity.

Note this also fires when *any* applier called `_furar_lock_de_emulacao` (which sets `-inf`), i.e. **any manual `profile.switch` in between kills the pendency**.
3. `lifecycle.py:2262-2271` — `store.active_profile != pendencia.profile_name` → discarded, `motivo=perfil_ativo_mudou`. With autoswitch at 2 Hz this is very easy to hit.
4. `lifecycle.py:2272-2282` — `display_authority == "game"` AND `_modo_seria_destrutivo(mode)` (`:2207-2231`) → **held indefinitely**, logs `profile_mode_pendencia_aguardando_jogo` once (`esperando_jogo` dedupe). It is released only when authority leaves `"game"`.

# Cross-cutting conclusions for your two symptoms

**"Profiles get overwritten during gameplay"** — three independent paths, none of them rate-limited by the 30 s lock:
1. `autoswitch.py:303-307` fires on any focus flip; the catch-all (`vitoria`) has `mode=None` and, while `apply_profile_mode`/`apply_profile_suppression` do guard against it (`lifecycle.py:1931`, `:1574`), **`apply_profile_rumble_policy` has no catch-all guard at all** — hence `profile_rumble_policy_reverted` in your journal.
2. `lifecycle.py:1911-1913` — every non-`game_signal` activation nulls `_modo_jogo_padrao`, so the very next `_sincronizar_modo_jogo_padrao` tick re-runs `aplicar_modo_jogo_padrao` from scratch.
3. `connection.py:128` re-writes the speaker on every reconnect.

**"Activating a profile does not immediately apply the tabs' settings"** — two distinct mechanisms:
1. **Untimed** `manual_override_categories` blanking trigger/led fields into `None` at `manager.py:341-348` (log: `profile_apply_respeita_override_manual`). Only `profile.switch`/hotkey/game-rule clear it, and `ipc_handlers.py:430` / `hotkey.py:168` were only recently moved before `activate` (TRAVA-QUE-SOLTA-TARDE-01, `ipc_handlers.py:399-417`) — the **autoswitch path still has no such clear for non-game profiles**.
2. **30 s** `_emu_manual_ts` lock deferring `mode`/`mouse`/`suppression`/`rumble_policy`, where only `mode` ever retries, and that retry has four ways to die (`lifecycle.py:2252-2282`) — most commonly guard 3 (`perfil_ativo_mudou`) at 2 Hz autoswitch cadence, and guard 4 (held forever while `display_authority == "game"` and the change is destructive).
